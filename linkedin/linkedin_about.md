I learn quickly, enjoy unfamiliar technical challenges, and tend to gravitate toward projects that require untangling complexity and improving clarity.

I’m also a published technical author, longtime pool instructor, science fiction writer, and lifelong believer that teaching others is one of the best ways to deepen understanding.

Technology changes constantly. Curiosity, problem-solving, communication, and adaptability continue to matter.
