# Professional Positioning

## Core Identity

A systems-oriented application developer with a strong UX and front-end focus who specializes in modernization, operational workflows, and solving messy technical problems.

---

## Key Differentiators

- Tool-agnostic developer with a history of rapid technical onboarding
- Strong blend of creativity and logical systems thinking
- Experienced in both front-end UX and broader application architecture
- Operational problem solver who enjoys untangling difficult systems
- Published technical author and longtime teacher/instructor
- Human-centered approach to software usability and workflow design
- Comfortable learning new technologies quickly when needed
- Strong collaborator and knowledge-sharing mindset

---

## Ideal Environment

Thrives in environments that value:

- autonomy and trust
- practical problem-solving
- work-life balance
- collaboration without micromanagement
- modernization and continuous learning
- thoughtful UX and maintainable systems
- engineers with broad technical curiosity

---

## Career Themes

Repeated themes throughout career progression:

- self-directed learning
- modernization efforts
- operational systems
- UX improvements
- technical adaptation
- troubleshooting and optimization
- collaborative problem-solving
- teaching and knowledge-sharing
- architecture and workflow thinking

---

## AI Philosophy

Views AI as an accelerator for collaboration, problem-solving, documentation, and developer productivity — not as a replacement for human judgment, architecture, creativity, or systems thinking.

Strong believer in using AI-assisted workflows to reduce repetitive effort while preserving thoughtful engineering practices and human-centered design.
