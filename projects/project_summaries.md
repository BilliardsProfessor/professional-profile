# Project Summaries

## Workload Distribution Tool (WDT)

A county-wide operational platform designed to distribute workloads fairly and efficiently across eligibility and social work staff.

Originally developed by an outside vendor before the project was reassigned internally. Assumed ownership mid-project and led continued development, optimization, modernization, and operational support.

### Highlights

- Supported approximately 1,000 users
- Integrated with CalWIN operational systems
- Balanced workloads using availability, specialization, and bilingual capabilities
- Improved Oracle-backed query performance from minutes/hours to seconds
- Maintained long-term operational stability with minimal application defects

## STARS.NEXT

An evolving AI-driven knowledge management initiative intended to support county-wide employee workflows and information retrieval.

Currently focused on front-end modernization, UX improvements, reusable standards, maintainability, and human-centered workflow design.

### Highlights

- Azure-oriented modernization initiative
- Front-end UX and standards development
- Internal operational workflow focus
- AI-assisted knowledge management direction
- Reusable component and maintainability planning

## CFS Wraparound System

Internal operational platform supporting foster care and child welfare service/payment tracking.

### Highlights

- Supported operational staff workflows
- Managed data affecting thousands of children
- Focused on usability and operational clarity
- Designed and developed internally

---

## Trust Fund Management System

Financial tracking system designed to monitor and manage trust fund allocations for children under county care.

### Highlights

- Integrated operational and banking workflows
- Supported trust allocation visibility and tracking
- Focused on reliability and maintainability
