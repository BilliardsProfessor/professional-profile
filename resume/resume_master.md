## Selected Systems & Projects

### Workload Distribution Tool (WDT)
**Contra Costa County EHSD**

Assumed ownership of a critical workload distribution platform after the original vendor was removed mid-project. Led continued development, optimization, and modernization efforts for a .NET / MS SQL application supporting approximately 1,000 eligibility and social work staff.

Designed workload balancing logic based on worker availability, specialization, and bilingual capabilities while integrating with CalWIN operational data systems. Reduced major Oracle-backed query bottlenecks from minute/hour-scale delays to seconds through extensive application and query optimization.

WDT remained operationally stable for years with very low defect rates, with most support issues ultimately traced to infrastructure or upstream data/network problems rather than application instability.
